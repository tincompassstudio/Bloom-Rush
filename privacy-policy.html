Privacy Policy — Bloomrush

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Tin Compass Studio (“we”, “us”) develops Bloomrush (the “Game”) for Android and iOS. This policy explains what data the Game and the third-party services inside it collect, and what we do and do not have access to.

Summary

We do not run any server and we do not receive your personal data. Your game progress stays on your device. The Game does include third-party services that collect data of their own, described below:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) and Google User Messaging Platform (ad consent), on Android and iOS
  • Google Play Billing (purchases on Android)
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit (purchases on iOS)

Data stored on your device

The Game saves the following locally, using your platform's standard app storage. It never leaves your device, we cannot see it, and uninstalling the Game deletes it:

  • Campaign progress (sectors unlocked and cleared, stars earned)
  • In-game currency balance (“spores”), boosters, upgrades and themes you own
  • Your chosen Core name and selected theme
  • Settings: audio volume, difficulty, reduce motion and Daily reminder
  • Your Daily Challenge streak
  • Which purchases the Game has applied, so it can grant them (and remove them if a purchase is refunded)
  • A per-day count of rewarded ads watched
  • Which tips and story scenes you have already seen

Advertising — Google AdMob

The Game shows two kinds of ad: occasional full-screen ads between levels (never shown if you own Ad-Free or Complete), and optional “watch an ad to earn spores” rewarded ads that only play when you choose them.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects data directly — we never see it. Depending on your settings and region, AdMob may collect:

  • Your advertising identifier — the Android Advertising ID, or on iOS the IDFA, only if you allow tracking when Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language, coarse region)
  • IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, viewed, clicked)

This is used to deliver and measure ads and, where you allow it, to personalise them. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy & Terms.

Consent and tracking choices

  • Ad consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the Game shows Google's consent form before any ads are requested. Where you are entitled to revisit that choice, the Game's Settings → Privacy options reopens it at any time.
  • Tracking on iOS. The Game asks once, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether ads may use your device's advertising identifier. Choosing “Ask App Not to Track” still plays the whole Game; ads are simply less relevant. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Purchases

The Game offers two optional one-time purchases: Ad-Free and Complete. There are no subscriptions and nothing is sold for real money inside a level; boosters are earned with in-game spores. Payments are processed by Google Play on Android and by Apple on iOS.

We never receive or store your payment details. We do not see your card number, billing address, name or account details. The Game receives only a confirmation from the store that a specific product was purchased, which it uses to grant the item. Purchase history and payment data are handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies.

Refunds are decided by Google Play or Apple under their own policies. If a purchase is refunded, the Game removes what that purchase granted — including its themes and any boosters from its starter kit that have not been used.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional Daily reminder, the Game asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your device (at most a few days ahead) to tell you when a new Daily Challenge is ready. They are created locally: no notification service, server or identifier is involved, and nothing is sent off your device. Turn them off in the Game's Settings → Notifications or in your device settings.

What we do not collect

We operate no server and no account system. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or files. The Game contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting, no social features and no chat.

Children's privacy

Bloomrush is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It contains no mature content. Because the Game includes advertising and in-app purchases, we ask that a parent or guardian supervise purchases made by minors. Google Play's purchase authentication and Apple's Ask to Buy and Screen Time offer controls for this.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the advertising service, contact us and we will assist you in raising it with Google.
