Privacy Policy — Bloomrush

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Tin Compass Studio (“we”, “us”) develops Bloomrush (the “Game”). This policy explains what data the Game and the third-party services inside it collect, and what we do and do not have access to.

Summary

We do not run any server and we do not receive your personal data. Your game progress stays on your device. However, the Game does include two Google services — Google AdMob (advertising) and Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) — and those services collect data of their own, described below.

Data stored on your device

The Game saves the following locally, using your platform's standard app storage. It never leaves your device, we cannot see it, and uninstalling the Game deletes it:

  • Campaign progress (sectors unlocked and cleared)
  • In-game currency balance (“spores”) and owned items
  • Cosmetic settings and your chosen Core name
  • Booster inventory, loadout and upgrade levels
  • Audio preferences
  • Whether ads have been removed via purchase, and a per-day count of rewarded ads watched

Advertising — Google AdMob

The Game shows two kinds of ad: occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional “watch an ad to earn spores” rewarded ads that only play when you choose them.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects data directly — we never see it. Depending on your settings and region, AdMob may collect:

  • Your advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language, coarse region)
  • IP address
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, viewed, clicked)

This is used to deliver and measure ads and, unless you opt out, to personalise them. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy & Terms. You can limit it in Settings → Google → Ads on your device, where you can delete or reset your advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation.

Purchases — Google Play Billing

The Game offers optional in-app purchases (spore packs and a one-time “Ad-Free” purchase). All payments are processed by Google Play.

We never receive or store your payment details. We do not see your card number, billing address, or name. The Game receives only a confirmation from Google that a specific product was purchased, which it uses to grant the item. Purchase history and payment data are handled by Google under their own privacy policy.

What we do not collect

We operate no server and no account system. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or files. The Game contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting, no social features and no chat.

Children's privacy

Bloomrush is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It contains no mature content. Because the Game includes advertising and in-app purchases, we ask that a parent or guardian supervise purchases made by minors. Google Play offers purchase authentication controls for this.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the advertising service, contact us and we will assist you in raising it with Google.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to export or delete. For the data Google collects through AdMob and Play Billing, use Google's own privacy controls:

If you are a California resident, note that we do not sell personal information; Google's own disclosures cover data it collects.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above changes with it. For material changes — such as adding a service that collects new categories of data — the change will be described in the release notes for the app version that introduces it.
